I have owned a ranking system in production

The recommendation engine at Crafto taught me what the metrics never show: cold-start pain, feedback loops, the drift between what a system optimises and what users actually want.

Fluent with data teams

I partnered daily with data science on seeding, cohorts, and evaluation. I do not need the model explained twice, and I know when a plausible number is hiding a broken pipeline.

Experimentation is my default

Every lift on this page came from a program of experiments, not a launch. pLTV lives or dies on rigorous evaluation cadence: gated rollouts, holdouts, honest reads.

Instrument first, argue never

Every feature I ship is data-instrumented before launch. Predictions are only as good as their ground truth, and clean ground truth is a product habit, not a data-team request.
